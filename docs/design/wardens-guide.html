Lodgy · for the warden running the place

Everything you need to run your hostel from your phone.

This guide walks through Lodgy screen by screen, in the order you'd actually use them — from setting a PIN on day one, to the daily habit of checking who owes rent. Nothing here needs the internet: all your data stays on this phone.

01 · Auth

Getting started

The first time you open Lodgy, you'll set a PIN — this is what keeps the app locked on your phone. From then on, unlock it with the same PIN, or your fingerprint if your phone supports it.

1
Create a PIN
Pick 4–6 digits. You'll confirm it once more on the next screen.
2
Unlock on open
Every time you open Lodgy after that, enter your PIN or tap the fingerprint icon.
02 · Property Setup

Setting up your property

Before you can move in a single tenant, tell Lodgy what you're managing: your hostel, its floors, its rooms, and the beds inside each room. Run more than one hostel? Switch between them any time from "Your Hostels."

1
Add your hostel
Name, address, contact number. Tap + to add another property later.
2
Fill in the details
This is what tenants and reports refer to it by.
3
Add floors
Ground, 1st, 2nd — however your building is laid out.
4
Add rooms to a floor
Each room gets a type — single, double, triple — and a price per bed.
5
Or add several at once
Setting up a whole floor? Bulk-add rooms with the same type and price in one go.
6
Beds, tracked individually
Open a room to see each bed's status — this is what makes vacancy tracking accurate.
03 · Tenant Onboarding

Bringing in a new tenant

The Tenants tab shows everyone currently living with you. To onboard someone new: pick a vacant bed, capture their profile and ID proof, then set the rent and move-in terms. The bed flips to occupied the moment you save.

1
Open Tenants
Search anyone by name or phone. Tap + to start onboarding.
2
Pick a vacant bed
Browse by floor and room — occupied beds are greyed out.
3
Capture their profile
Photo, ID proof, and an emergency contact — take these from the camera or gallery.
4
Set the agreement
Rent, deposit, billing day. Moving in someone who's already been living there? Backdate it.
5
Their profile is ready
Call, WhatsApp or SMS them straight from here, any time.
04 · Rent & Payments

Collecting rent

Lodgy generates each tenant's invoice on their billing day automatically. Record what you actually collect — in full or in part — and send a reminder pre-filled on WhatsApp or SMS; you always review and tap send yourself. When a tenant leaves, checkout settles their deposit.

1
See who owes what
Filter by unpaid, partial or paid across all your tenants.
2
Record a payment
Cash, UPI, bank transfer — full or partial, the invoice status updates itself.
3
Send a reminder
Pick WhatsApp or SMS and a language — the message is pre-filled, you just tap send.
4
Backfill an old invoice
For a tenant you onboarded mid-tenancy, so their history is correct from day one.
5
Check a tenant out
Deduct any damages, settle the refund, and the bed frees up automatically.
05 · Reporting & Dashboard

Checking in on your hostel

Home is your morning check — today's collections, overdue invoices, vacant beds, upcoming move-outs, all in one glance. Drill into every vacant bed, or pull a monthly report of income, dues and occupancy for the whole hostel.

1
Your home screen
The four numbers that matter each morning, plus who's moving out soon.
2
Every vacant bed
Grouped by floor, so you know exactly what's free to show a walk-in.
3
Monthly report
Collected, dues, occupancy, and income against expense — per hostel.
4
Export it
PDF or CSV, for your own records or to hand off. Arriving in a later release.
06 · Tenant Notes

Keeping notes on a tenant

Log complaints, damage, or general notes against a tenant's own timeline. Each note remembers the date the thing actually happened, not just when you got around to typing it in.

1
Their timeline
Every note, sorted by when it happened.
2
Add a note
Attach a photo for damage reports. Editable and deletable any time.
07 · Expenses

Tracking what you spend

Log expenses against a hostel — wifi, electricity, repairs — and mark the recurring ones so next month's bill is one tap away.

1
This month's spending
Everything logged for the hostel, newest first.
2
Add an expense
Pick a category, the amount, and whether it repeats monthly.
08 · Backup & Restore

Backing up your data

Lodgy keeps everything on your phone — no cloud, no account. Export a backup whenever you like and keep the file somewhere safe; restore it if you ever switch phones.

1
Export a backup
One zip with your database and every tenant photo. Save it to Downloads, Drive, or an SD card.
2
Restore on a new phone
Pick your backup file — this replaces everything currently on the phone, so make sure it's the right one.
09 · Settings

Settings

Switch the app's language, change your PIN, turn on fingerprint unlock, or jump to backups and your hostels — all from one place.

1
Everything in one place
Lodgy defaults to Hindi on first launch — switch to English any time.
